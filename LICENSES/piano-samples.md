Keylight bundles 16 unmodified MP3 files in renderer/samples/salamander/.
This is a compact single-velocity subset of the distributor's encoded samples,
covering A0-C8 with nearest-root transposition (at most three semitones within
that range). It is not the full 16-layer Salamander library. Velocity changes
gain and low-pass brightness; held notes use the recorded natural decay without
looping. Key/pedal release applies a smooth damping envelope. No release-noise
or resonance samples are included. Runtime peak normalization, filtering and
pitch shifting do not alter the distributed files. No ROLI sounds are used.

Retain this attribution and the accompanying license files in distributions.
Parent integration must add this credit to THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md and include
LICENSES/piano-samples* in packaged licenses.
