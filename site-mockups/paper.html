01 Follow the light

See the note.
Find the key.

Notes fall toward the keys. Your keyboard lights up to show you what to play.

Works with LUMI Keys and ROLI Piano M light-up keyboards.

  1. Watch first.

    See how a song goes before you play.

  2. Take your time.

    Wait mode pauses the song until you play the right note. Or slow it to 0.5x or 0.75x.

  3. Stay with a phrase.

    Loop a section and give it another go.

57 songs to begin with.

Have something in mind? Import your own MIDI or MusicXML files.
