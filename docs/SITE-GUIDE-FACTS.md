# Facts for the Keylight guide (source of truth; state nothing that is not here)

## Getting started
- Keylight is free and open source (MIT). No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. Songs, recordings and progress stay on your device.
- Mac: Apple silicon, macOS 13 or newer. Windows 10/11 64-bit (beta). Android 8.0 or newer (beta, installed from an APK file). iPhone and iPad: coming soon.
- Keyboards: LUMI Keys and ROLI Piano M over USB (24 keys). On Android also over Bluetooth. A 49-key ROLI Piano works (beta). Two 24-key keyboards can be joined as one 48-key row, with two USB cables or snapped together with one cable (beta).
- On a Mac the keys light in full colour and the keyboard's arrow and mode buttons work. On Windows the keys light from the notes; these are not available on Windows yet: brightness slider, the light show after a song, find-the-keys, battery level, arrow and mode buttons. On Windows close ROLI Connect or any music software using the keyboard first.
- First start: a short setup checks sound, keys and lights. It can be run again from Settings.
- No keyboard? You can still look around.

## Playing a song
- Open Search, pick a song. 76 built-in songs: 19 easy arrangements and 57 classical pieces.
- Speed track at the top: Wait, 0.5x, 0.75x, Full speed. In Wait the song stops until you play the right note.
- Watch: the app plays it for you first.
- Hands: left, right or both. Labels: off, note names or finger numbers.
- Loop: mark a section and repeat it. Click: a metronome. Count-in before the song starts.
- Octave down and up: the − and + buttons, or Z and X.
- Views: Cascade, Rainbow, Colornote, Classic, and Sheet only (sheet music fills the stage; available for songs that have a score).
- Guided practice (button below the keyboard): the song is split into phrases, hands separately first, Wait mode first and then faster steps. It brings back the bars you got wrong.
- After a song you get a result: accuracy, timing, stars, longest streak.
- Instruments: pick from the drop-down in the toolbar: grand piano, a sampled concert grand, electric pianos, organ, harpsichord, marimba, nylon guitar, flute, cello, strings, bass, lead and pads.
- Expression (Settings > Keyboard lights > Pitch bend & pressure): wiggle a key to bend the pitch; press into a held key to swell sustained sounds.

## Adding your own songs
- Search > Add a song, or drop a file anywhere on the window.
- Recordings: MP3, M4A, WAV, FLAC, AAC, OGG, AIFF, up to 20 minutes. Keylight listens to the recording and writes the notes, on your computer. The file is never uploaded. Works best with piano or solo recordings.
- "Simplify to melody + bass" keeps the top line and a bass line, for an easier version.
- The original recording plays along in sync, and keeps its pitch at 0.5x and 0.75x.
- MIDI (.mid, .midi) and MusicXML (.xml, .musicxml, .mxl) are read as written. Sheet music is shown for MusicXML songs only.
- Title, artist and cover come from the file. On Mac and Windows, "Find cover art and lyrics online" can look them up (it sends only title, artist and length). Drop a .lrc file onto an open song to add lyrics.
- Your songs are marked YOURS in the library. Hover a tile to remove one.
- Correct notes: an editor to fix pitch, time, length, hand, finger and tempo, with Add, Remove and Undo. Editing a built-in song makes your own copy.
- YouTube and Spotify links are not supported.

## Course
- 12 levels, 94 short lessons, from finding middle C. Each level ends with an exam.
- Course order: Guided (asks before you jump ahead) or Open (go anywhere). Nothing is locked.
- Placement test and "skip level" for people who already play.

## Practice tab
- Ear training, Rhythm, Sight-reading, Play it back: five difficulty levels each, with your best result kept.
- Scales & chords, Games, a Metronome, the Playground (free play with any sound, a scale lit on the keys).
- Practice counts toward your daily goal (5 to 30 minutes) and streak.

## Jam
- Four tracks (drums, bass, chords, lead), four loop slots each. Record a loop; it starts and ends on the bar.
- Drum step grid, tempo, swing, metronome, delay and reverb, scale lock, snap to scale.
- Export the session as a MIDI file.

## Recording your playing
- Settings > Sound > Record my takes. Records the falling notes, the keyboard and the app's sound as a video.
- Mac: Movies > Keylight. Windows: Videos > Keylight. A take stops by itself after 15 minutes.

## Camera coach (experimental)
- Settings > Hand tracking > Start camera, then Find the keys (Mac) or Click the end keys.
- Shows numbered finger badges over the keys: 1 thumb to 5 little finger, blue for the left hand, orange for the right.
- Advisory finger coaching compares your fingering with the suggested one. It never changes your score.
- Runs on your computer; no video leaves it. The camera is only asked for when you press Start camera.
- Needs the whole keyboard in view. A phone on a stand above the keys works best; a laptop camera works poorly.
- Limits: it cannot tell hovering from pressing, and accuracy drops at steep angles.

## Airwave (experimental)
- Keylight can use the Airwave's hand-gesture MIDI (raise, tilt, grasp) to shape the sound: Settings > External instruments & gestures.
- Finger tracking from the Airwave is being tested with volunteers and is not confirmed to work.

## Support my device
- Settings > Support my device. For keyboards that are missing, unlit or only half working.
- Five steps: Detect, Test, About you, Review, Send. It builds a plain-text report: MIDI ports, how the keyboard identifies itself, a 10-second key test (statistics only), an optional lights check.
- Nothing is sent automatically. You copy it, save it, or open a pre-filled GitHub issue.
- Never included: serial numbers, file paths, user names, song titles, recordings, progress, the notes you played.

## Backups
- Settings > Progress: save your library, settings and progress to one .keylightbackup file, and restore it later or on another computer.
- Keylight also keeps the last three daily automatic backups.
